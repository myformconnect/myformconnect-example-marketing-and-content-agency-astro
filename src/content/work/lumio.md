## The Challenge

Lumio had built a next-generation corporate treasury management platform capable of consolidating multi-currency liquidity in real time. While their product was exceptional, their customer acquisition model was dangerously reliant on paid search and outbound enterprise sales reps.

With cost-per-click rates in the B2B financial software vertical soaring beyond £42 per visitor, customer acquisition costs were unsustainable. Lumio’s existing website suffered from structural indexation problems, thin product comparison pages, and zero presence for high-intent search terms evaluated by Chief Financial Officers and corporate treasurers.

> "We were spending hundreds of thousands every quarter on Google Ads to capture buyers who should have found us naturally. We needed an organic engine that CFOs actually respected."

## The Strategic Approach

WESTERVANE conducted a deep forensic audit of the European corporate treasury landscape. We identified that modern finance teams do not search for generic software buzzwords; they search for technical compliance solutions, regulatory transitions (such as ISO 20022), and clear comparative frameworks.

Our strategy focused on three pillars:

1. **Foundational Information Architecture**: Restructuring 180+ URLs into discrete thematic clusters around liquidity forecasting, FX risk management, and banking API integrations.
2. **Authoritative Editorial Thought Leadership**: Partnering with former treasury executives to write exhaustive white papers, benchmarking reports, and workflow playbooks.
3. **Programmatic Comparison Hubs**: Creating data-backed alternative pages comparing Lumio against legacy banking software and manual spreadsheet workflows.

## The Solution & Execution

Over nine months, our multidisciplinary team worked alongside Lumio's product specialists and engineering leads:

- **Technical SEO Remediation**: Re-architected their headless web stack, eliminating severe client-side rendering bottlenecks and improving Core Web Vitals to 98+ on mobile and desktop.
- **The Treasury Dispatch**: Launched an editorial research portal featuring original quantitative surveys on liquidity trends across 400 European mid-market finance directors.
- **Conversion-Optimized Templates**: Redesigned long-form case studies and interactive ROI calculators that guided corporate finance teams seamlessly from organic discovery to private product demonstrations.

## The Results

Within ten months of deployment, Lumio established organic search dominance in the UK and DACH regions:

- **+310% increase in inbound organic pipeline value**, directly contributing to their Series B milestone.
- **First-page search visibility** for 48 competitive high-intent terms including *multi-entity treasury automation* and *real-time liquidity visibility*.
- **Over £1.8M in annualized paid ad efficiency**, allowing Lumio to redirect capital toward core platform engineering.
