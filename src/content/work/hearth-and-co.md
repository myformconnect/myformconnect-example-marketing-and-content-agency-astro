## The Challenge

Hearth & Co. has spent two decades manufacturing timeless architectural hardware, ceramics, and sustainable textiles in Somerset and the Cotswolds. Despite their uncompromising commitment to materials and craft, their digital brand presence had fallen out of step with how younger, design-conscious homeowners discover and furnish living spaces.

Their digital touchpoints felt fragmented, their online store experienced high cart abandonment, and their marketing relied predominantly on infrequent retail trade exhibitions and seasonal wholesale orders.

> "We knew our objects possessed soul and enduring beauty. What we lacked was a cohesive digital voice that could convey that tactility across digital screens."

## The Strategic Approach

WESTERVANE was commissioned to execute a holistic brand elevation and omni-channel digital campaign:

1. **Refined Editorial Narrative**: Shifting the positioning from "rustic traditional homeware" to "timeless architectural living," appealing to aesthetic purists in London, Paris, and New York.
2. **Immersive Digital Vignettes**: Moving away from static, cut-out e-commerce product photos toward richly textured interior portraits, capturing natural light, raw linen, and patinated brass in lived-in spaces.
3. **The "Considered Living" Publication**: Launching a dedicated digital editorial hub exploring sustainable architecture, profiles of master woodworkers, and architectural lighting essays.

## The Solution & Execution

We developed an integrated Autumn/Winter campaign titled *"Objects of Permanence"*:

- **Editorial Digital Lookbook**: Built an ultra-fast, responsive interactive lookbook featuring subtle scroll interactions and micro-narratives behind every hand-finished piece.
- **Targeted Paid Social & Search Campaigns**: Engineered nuanced creative campaigns across Pinterest, Meta, and Google Performance Max, pairing documentary-style video vignettes with high-intent home design search queries.
- **Lifecycle Retention Sequencing**: Designed a multi-part onboarding email experience that introduced customers to the provenance of their purchase, care guides, and exclusive invitations to limited studio releases.

## The Results

The repositioning redefined Hearth & Co.’s direct-to-consumer trajectory:

- **185% year-on-year increase in direct e-commerce revenue**, substantially reducing dependence on wholesale third-party distributors.
- **6.4x blended return on ad spend (ROAS)** across seasonal campaigns.
- **Over 42,000 highly engaged readers subscribed** to the weekly "Considered Home" publication, with an industry-leading 54% sustained open rate.
